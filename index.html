About the league

Real pool, played the right way

Ultimate Pool League Atlanta brings competitive amateur pool to bars and pool halls around the metro area. Teams play weekly league nights in 8-Ball and 10-Ball divisions, and the best earn a shot at the Grand Finals.

Matches run 30 minutes with a 30-second shot clock, so nights move quickly and nobody sits around waiting. Come with a full team, or sign up on your own and we'll place you with one.

Fun

League night is a social night with friends, teammates and rivals.

Fair

Clear formats, a shot clock and published rules for every match.

Competitive

Standings, qualifiers and a path to the Grand Finals.

Real pool

Real players, real tables, every skill level welcome.

Join the league

Bring a cue. We'll find you a team.

New to league play, coming back for another session, or putting a team together? Pick how you're joining and fill out the form. It takes about a minute.

What happens after you register

  1. We review your sign-up and match you with a league night and venue near you.
  2. A league coordinator contacts you by phone or email with your team, schedule and fees.
  3. You show up and play on your first league night. Bring your cue, or use a house cue.
